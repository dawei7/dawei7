Study the book. Write the proof.

Professor Euler is a free, non-commercial study desk for mathematics and every STEM subject. Bring your textbooks, lecture notes, exercise sheets and recorded lectures, keep the page in view, and write your work by hand right beside it. When you ask, your own AI provider checks the solution you choose to send.

Get it on Google Play Download for Windows What the app does

Bring

Import PDFs one by one or a whole folder: textbooks, lecture notes, slides, exercise sheets, past exams. My books keeps them in folders, one for each course, and reading works offline.

Write

Frame any exercise on a page and it gets its own handwriting sheet, with the task shown large above it. Write with a stylus or your finger, on a tablet or a phone, and annotate the page itself.

Check

AI grading looks at the handwritten solution you submit, step by step, and asks a guiding question instead of giving the answer away. It can miss a step, and it is not a formal mark. You bring your own provider key.

Note

Canvas, LaTeX and Markdown notes, on real paper sizes, exported as PDF. Open a Markdown file from your computer and read it typeset, mathematics included, or edit it.

Watch

Add video and audio lectures and write notes at timestamps while you watch; each note takes you back to its moment. On Android, your own study music plays in the app too.

Keep going

Statistics shows your study time, this week against the last, days in a row, your year at a glance and how far you are through each book. An empty day is a rest, not a failure.

Celebrate with other learners

Share your successes, find study partners and ask about the app in the Professor Euler community on Discord: an exercise you finally solved, a streak, a passed exam.

Every book in the library has its own post in #book-talk, and #app-feedback takes bugs, ideas and questions. The community is for adults, like the app. Discord’s terms and privacy policy apply there; the app sends nothing to Discord.

A free library in five study paths

The Professor Euler Library holds 38 open-licensed books and courses in English. Seven come with the app; the others download for free, one book or a whole path at a time. You study the authors’ text, and every book keeps its own license.

Algebra, Geometry & Precalculus

Build the algebra, geometry and function skills that make university mathematics feel approachable.

Pure Mathematics

Move from calculating examples to proving general statements about structures, spaces and functions.

Applied Mathematics

Turn a mathematical model into a calculation you can implement, test and explain.

AI Research

Probability, statistics and machine learning: how learning systems are built, why they can generalize, and how to investigate when they fail.

Complete Mathematics

A broad mathematical education that connects proof, computation, uncertainty and learning.

Among the books: OpenStax Calculus, Richard Hammack’s Book of Proof, Sheldon Axler’s Linear Algebra Done Right and Measure, Integration & Real Analysis, and Simon J. D. Prince’s Understanding Deep Learning. The library is a starting point; your own material comes first.

No Professor Euler account

Profiles live on the device. There is no subscription and no advertising, and the app speaks 21 languages. Optional AI uses DeepSeek, OpenRouter or DeepInfra with a key you provide; that provider’s charges and terms apply.

Google sign-in is optional and off until you turn on sync for a profile. It reaches only a hidden folder in your own Google Drive, plus the email address of that account, so your other devices can open the same study files and notes. The library’s books are not uploaded. API keys stay on the device. The privacy policy is the full account of this, and the same text is published at github.com/dawei7/professor-euler-privacy.

The same study on Windows

Professor Euler for Windows is the same workspace: My books, frames and Check, notes, recordings and Statistics. The seven included books are inside the installer, and Get books downloads the rest. Double-click it, open the app from the Start menu, and start. You can write with a mouse or a pen.

Version 4.4.1, for Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit; the installer is about 300 MB. No account is required to study. Sign in to the same Google account only if you want a profile to meet your phone or tablet. When a newer version is published on this page, the app says so. It downloads the installer only after you press Update, and it checks the file before opening it. The installer is not signed with a paid certificate, so Windows may show a warning. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

Made in Switzerland

David Schmid built Professor Euler for the way he wants to study: keep the depth, and make the desk comfortable. The tutoring voice in the app is a fictional tribute to Leonhard Euler, not the historical man.

Android 10 or later, on a phone or a tablet; a stylus is welcome, a finger works too. Questions: ai.professor.euler@gmail.com.
