Privacy policy - Professor Euler

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Who provides the app

Professor Euler is a free, non-commercial mathematics study app for adults aged 18 and older, created by David Schmid. Contact: ai.professor.euler@gmail.com. This policy describes the Android app and the Windows app. Google Play and services you choose to use have their own policies.

Work stored on your device

The app stores, on your device: local profile names; the books and documents in My books (PDFs and prepared books you import, books from the Professor Euler Library, canvas and LaTeX notes, and video and audio recordings you import); the exercise, question, bookmark and solution frames on their pages; handwriting and typed work on sheets; PDF annotations; the sections you mark as read; AI check results and Ask Euler conversations; settings; and local AI usage and cost totals. Imported files are copied into the app's private storage; the originals are not changed. Profiles are local and do not create an online account. The developer does not receive this work. Avoid putting personal or sensitive information in work you intend to send to an AI service.

The Statistics tab works out study days, streaks, grades and progress through each book from this local work. The app records aggregate study time by date and document on your device while a document is open and you have touched the screen or written in the last five minutes; it does not count in the background. It does not store a raw touch log or send activity statistics to the developer. Time records are profile-specific and included in ZIP backups; deleting a profile removes its records. Historical study duration from before this feature is not reconstructed.

Music

The Music section plays songs you import yourself. They are copied into the app's private storage on this device, with the playlists and the last playing position, and are kept for the whole device rather than a profile. They are never sent to the developer and are not part of ZIP backups. They go to Google Drive only if you turn on Sync recordings and music too (see Optional Google Drive sync below). Music plays while the app is on screen. Only if you turn on Keep music playing in the background (Settings > Reading & focus) does the app hold a media-playback foreground service while music plays, so it keeps playing when the screen is off or another app is open; Android then shows the song's title and artist in the media notification and on the lock screen. The original files you picked are not changed.

Optional AI features: DeepSeek, OpenRouter and DeepInfra

Reading, writing, marking and saving work do not need an AI connection. You choose DeepSeek, OpenRouter or DeepInfra in Settings and supply your own API key for that provider. Nothing is sent until you press Check or send a question to Ask Euler. When you press Check, the framed exercise (as an image of that region and its page), any context region you marked for it, its linked solution if one is available, and your work sheet are sent directly to the selected provider over HTTPS with your key. When you ask Euler, your question, the earlier messages of that conversation, the page or framed region (with its context, if you marked one) and, if you attach it, your work sheet are sent; neighbouring pages are added only when the question needs them. Pressing Apply in Settings makes an authenticated connection check to the selected provider before saving the key and choice; this check sends no study work. The provider receives connection metadata such as your IP address. The developer does not operate an intermediary server.

DeepSeek requires a paid API key. DeepSeek processes the content sent to its API under the terms applicable to your account. Review https://platform.deepseek.com/ and its linked service and privacy terms before enabling it. DeepSeek's published privacy policy describes processing and storage in the People's Republic of China: https://cdn.deepseek.com/policies/en-US/deepseek-privacy-policy.html . Do not submit personal or sensitive information in your study work. The app cannot verify your provider billing or regional eligibility, control provider retention or training practices, or delete copies retained by a provider.

OpenRouter lets you choose a model and routes the submitted content to that model's hosting provider. Both OpenRouter and the hosting provider process the request under their applicable terms and your account settings; storage, training and location can differ by route. Review https://openrouter.ai/privacy , https://openrouter.ai/terms and https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/privacy/provider-logging before enabling it. The app does not certify user-selected models or change your OpenRouter privacy settings.

DeepInfra receives the content sent to the selected model under its service and model-specific terms. Data handling can vary by model and hosting arrangement. Review https://docs.deepinfra.com/account/data-privacy , https://deepinfra.com/privacy and https://deepinfra.com/terms before enabling it. The app does not change your DeepInfra account settings or certify user-selected models.

Each provider's eligibility, regional restrictions and charges apply. Availability of this app in a country does not guarantee that any AI provider can be used there. AI can make mistakes and is not a formal assessment authority.

Adult use

Professor Euler is intended for adults aged 18 and older. Before AI features are used, you confirm that you are 18 or older; the confirmation is stored on the device. The app does not collect a birth date or identity document and does not claim to verify your age. It is not intended for children or other users under 18.

API keys and usage records

Each provider has a separate saved API key. On Android it is encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore of your device. On Windows it is encrypted with a key stored in your Windows user profile, so anyone with access to that profile can read it. Keys are never written into ZIP backups or Google Drive, and you can remove them in Settings. Token totals, model IDs, reported charges and cost estimates stay on the device. API costs shows local usage only, not a complete provider bill; its reset controls remove the selected local totals and do not change provider records.

Backups and exports

Back up all work writes a ZIP file to a place you choose with the system file picker, such as this device or a cloud drive app. It contains the profile's library, the PDFs you imported, your study work and your study time. It leaves out API keys, settings, the books that come with the app (the app supplies them again on restore) and recordings (import the same file again to reattach its timestamps and notes). The ZIP is not password-encrypted; anyone with access to it can read its contents. Restoring adds the documents of the backup that are not already on the device to the current profile and does not overwrite work that is there. The app opts out of Android's system backup and device transfer, so its data is not copied into a system cloud backup.

You can export a book's pages (all pages, marked pages, exercises or bookmarks) or a canvas note as a PDF, and print or save a LaTeX note as a PDF through the system print dialog. Each export is saved where you choose. Exported PDFs are not password-encrypted; anyone you share one with can read its included work, and a PDF cannot be restored as editable study work. Exported files and backups remain until you delete them, including after uninstalling the app.

Optional book downloads

Get books downloads open-licensed books of the Professor Euler Library from its public repository on GitHub (github.com and GitHub's file hosts), and from time to time the library's catalog. A request contains no account, profile name, study work or other personal content; GitHub receives the request and your device's IP address, as for any web page, under GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement . The app checks every file against the published fingerprint before adding it. Nothing is downloaded until you ask for a book or a path, and the books that come with the app need no connection.

Optional Google Drive sync

Sync stays off until you turn on Sync this profile and sign in with Google. Professor Euler then asks Google only for access to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata) and for your email address, so the app can tell which Google account is connected. It does not request access to the rest of your Drive, Gmail, or Photos.

The profile you choose to sync can place study files in that hidden folder: the frames, sheets, annotations, reading marks, checks and conversations of your books, your own notes (canvas, LaTeX and Markdown notes: their contents, name and folder), study time, a few study preferences, and the PDFs you imported yourself. Those files use your Google account storage. Books of the Professor Euler Library are not uploaded: each device gets them from the app or the library. Recordings and music are uploaded only if you also turn on Sync recordings and music too (off by default): then the recordings' own video and audio files, your songs with their titles and artists, and your playlists are placed in the hidden folder as well, so another device signed in to the same Google account gets them; what is playing and where stays on each device. With that switch off, recordings are not uploaded; their timestamps and notes are, and appear on another device once the same file is imported there. A note made or imported on one device appears on the others, and removing it removes it there too. The developer does not receive these files. Drive's website does not list them as ordinary files. You open them again inside Professor Euler on a device where you sign in to the same Google account.

The newest saved copy of each study file is the one kept. API keys, the Google sign-in token, and device settings, including appearance, language, gestures, and calibration, stay on the device. They are not written into the hidden folder. Turning sync off does not remove the Google connection. Disconnect removes the saved sign-in from that device. Uninstalling the app does not delete the hidden folder. You can remove the app's access in your Google Account, and you can delete the hidden app data from Google Drive settings. Google's own policy and terms apply to your account and to files stored there: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/terms .

Contact and external links

You can email David at ai.professor.euler@gmail.com. He receives your email address and whatever you include, and uses it to respond and investigate the reported issue. Do not include API keys or private student information. Links in the app open GitHub, Google, Discord, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, DeepInfra and other external destinations, which apply their own privacy policies. Join on Discord opens the invitation to the Professor Euler learners' community in your browser or the Discord app. After a check with full marks, Share on Discord shows a post about it (the grade, the exercise, its section or page, the book's title and the beginning of the printed question); Copy, or opening the #wins channel, puts that post on your device's clipboard, and it is posted only if you paste and send it in Discord yourself. The app sends nothing to Discord, and what you post there is governed by Discord's terms and privacy policy. Contact David to request deletion of your correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Advertising and analytics

The app includes no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, subscription service or automatic developer telemetry. Optional AI processing, book downloads, Drive sync and the backups and exports you make are described above and are not a claim that the app never transmits data.

Your controls and deletion

Remove books, notes and recordings in My books and songs in Music, or delete a profile, to remove local work; clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove all of its private data. These actions do not delete ZIP backups or PDFs you saved elsewhere, shared copies, email correspondence, data held by your AI provider, or study files already stored in your Google Drive hidden app folder. Delete those separately through their respective services. Keep a separate backup of important work; no device or software can guarantee permanent storage.

Windows

On Windows, study files and saved API keys are kept in your user profile under Professor Euler. The app does not require an account. When the Windows app starts, it requests a small public version file from euler.dawei7.com to see whether a newer installer has been published. That request sends no name, study work, or Google account. The site does not keep an account of the check. If you press Update, the app downloads the installer from this same site and opens it only after the file matches the published fingerprint. Nothing is downloaded until you ask. AI features, book downloads and Drive sync work on Windows as described above.

Permissions

Internet access supports direct requests to the selected AI provider, books you download from the Professor Euler Library, optional Google sign-in and profile sync, and the Windows version check described above. Network-state access supports connection handling. While you complete Google sign-in in the browser, a short foreground service with a notification and a wake lock keep the app listening on this device for the sign-in to return. When you switch on background playback for music, a media-playback foreground service with its notification keeps it playing in the background. A long Google Drive sync goes on when you leave the app: from Android 14 as a user-initiated data transfer job, with a notification that shows its progress and can stop it, and on earlier versions as an ordinary background job. While recordings and music are kept to Wi-Fi (the default), their files wait on mobile data, and a background job syncs them once a connection without a data limit is available. Files are opened and saved through the system file picker; the app does not request broad access to all your files, location, contacts, microphone or camera.

Changes and questions

This policy will be updated if the app's data handling changes. For privacy questions, corrections or deletion of correspondence, email ai.professor.euler@gmail.com.
